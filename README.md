# 🎬 IMDb Movie Analysis Project

## 📁 Dataset

- **Source**: [IMDb Dataset of Top 1000 Movies and TV Shows](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/harshitshankhdhar/imdb-dataset-of-top-1000-movies-and-tv-shows)
- **Format**: CSV
- **Fields**: Title, Year, Genre, IMDb Rating, Director, Stars, Country, Budget, Gross Revenue

## ❓ Analysis Questions

1. Which genres have the highest average IMDb ratings?
2. Is there a correlation between a movie's budget and its gross revenue?
3. Who are the top 5 directors with the highest average movie ratings?
4. How has the average movie rating changed over the years?
5. What is the distribution of movie durations, and how does it relate to ratings?

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Excel Functions**: `AVERAGEIFS`, `CORREL`, `COUNTIFS`, `IF`, `VLOOKUP`
- **Data Cleaning**: Handled missing values, standardized genre names
- **Visualizations**: Bar charts, scatter plots, line graphs, combination charts

## 📊 Insights

- *Genres*: Drama and Documentary genres tend to have higher average ratings.
- *Budget vs. Revenue*: A strong positive correlation indicates that higher budgets often lead to higher gross revenues.
- *Top Directors*: Directors like Christopher Nolan and Quentin Tarantino have high average movie ratings.
- *Ratings Over Time*: Average movie ratings have shown slight fluctuations over the years.
- *Duration & Ratings*: Mid-length movies (90-120 minutes) often receive higher ratings.


## 📌 Conclusion

This project provides insights into factors influencing movie success on IMDb. Further analysis could include sentiment analysis of reviews or exploring the impact of star power on movie ratings.
